# C6 Career Intelligence

The career loop is now measurable without introducing a second job engine.

## Discovery
JobOps remains the discovery/search boundary. South African source policy records CareerJunction, PNet, LinkedIn and canonical employer ATS as attributable inputs. Imported records are deduplicated by canonical key and retain source/capture provenance.

## Outcome learning
Application and interview outcomes produce:
- application-to-interview conversion
- interview-to-final-outcome conversion
- source performance
- recurring skill/evidence gaps
- recurring role demand.

No score is treated as a truth claim about a candidate. The system uses observed records only.

## Mail intelligence
C6 Mail inbound events can be classified as application received, interview, offer, rejection or follow-up. The parser produces a proposal only. It never mutates the tracker and never sends a reply.

## Winner Effect
Every intelligence snapshot ends with a Target Win, Proof and Next Win so learning becomes an executable loop:
WIN -> ABSORB -> VERIFY -> SCALE.
